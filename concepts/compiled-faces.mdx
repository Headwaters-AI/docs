# Compiled Faces

A compiled face is more than a system prompt. It is a structured representation of a
persona, built from source texts, that can be retrieved and composed at inference
time rather than pasted in wholesale.

---

## What compiling does

When you upload source texts and trigger a sync, the compiler reads each document or
thread and reduces it to a set of compact, self-contained statements about the
persona.

Those statements are stored, indexed, and ranked. At inference time the ones relevant
to the current conversation are retrieved and injected — not the whole set, and not
the original text. A face with a large corpus and a face with a small one therefore
cost about the same per request; what grows is how much there is to choose from.

---

## Why fewer tokens

Raw source texts are verbose. A 10,000-token interview might be 80% context and
repetition, with the characterizing material concentrated in a small fraction of it.

Compiling strips the scaffolding. What reaches the model is not a summary or an
excerpt but a compact expression of what makes this persona distinctive, ranked by
relevance to what is being discussed right now.

The result conveys more character per token than a hand-written system prompt of
equivalent length, and it keeps doing so as the corpus grows.

---

## Why faces compose

Because compiled faces share a common structure, they can be combined with boolean
operators — union, intersection, difference — and the result is still a usable face.
That is what makes [synthetic faces](/guides/synthetic-faces) work: the operations
are well defined over compiled material in a way they would not be over raw prose.

---

## What makes a good source text

- **First-person material is strongest.** Interviews, journals, essays and sent
  messages carry voice as well as content.
- **Third-person material works** for biography and description — set
  `perspective=third-person` so it is read as being *about* the person rather than
  *by* them.
- **Volume helps, but distinctiveness helps more.** Ten pages where someone argues a
  position beat a hundred pages of logistics.
- **Declare what a document is** when you upload it. See
  [Compile](/guides/compile) — supplying `kind` produces a better result than
  leaving it out.
