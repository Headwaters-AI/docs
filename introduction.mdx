# Faces

Faces are compiled soul documents. They give AI agents programmable personas as nuanced as real humans using fewer tokens.

Source texts — interviews, essays, journals, transcripts, chat histories — are compiled into a compact representation of the persona. At inference time the parts relevant to the current conversation are retrieved and injected into the system prompt, conveying far more character per token than the raw text would.

## Key concepts

- **Face** — A named persona identified by its alias, compiled from source material. Can be a person, character, or synthetic blend of multiple faces.
- **Compile** — The process that turns uploaded source texts into a face, ready to be retrieved from at inference time.
- **Components** — What compiling produces: compact, retrievable pieces of a persona. Counts are reported per face as an array.
- **Synthetic faces** — Boolean combinations of existing faces (`alice | bob`, `expert & mentor`, etc.) composed on the fly without extra training.
